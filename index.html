What I build

Network, platform, security, observability, and resilience infrastructure — designed to stay understandable, recoverable, and honest about failure modes.

01

Network Architecture

From BGP topology decisions to VXLAN-EVPN fabric deployment — production-grade network design that accounts for failure modes before the first packet flows.

  • Routing & switching architecture — BGP, OSPF, VXLAN-EVPN, MPLS
  • Edge, WAN, and datacenter fabric design
  • Protocol migrations with zero-downtime execution strategy
  • DWDM inter-DC transport — wavelength-multiplexed dark fiber delivering hundreds of Gbps with per-service wavelength isolation, dual systems over geographically diverse fiber paths so a single fiber cut does not take down inter-site connectivity
  • IPv4/IPv6 dual-stack design and deployment — production IPv6 at the internet edge including DNS services, with active rollout ongoing
  • Hybrid cloud connectivity — AWS Direct Connect and Azure ExpressRoute terminated at the MPLS edge, bridging on-premises infrastructure to cloud workloads with dedicated private paths
02

Platform & Compute

Compute and virtualisation infrastructure built for the team who inherits it — HCI platforms, datacenter migrations, and capacity models that age gracefully.

  • HCI platform design and migration (Nutanix)
  • Datacenter decommission and consolidation strategy
  • Virtualisation architecture and capacity planning
03

Automation & Zero Trust

Removing humans from routine operations and implicit trust from the security model — at the same time, because they're the same problem.

  • Infrastructure as Code — Ansible, Terraform, Python
  • Zero Trust access architecture — SASE, Zscaler ZTE
  • Zero-drift operational model: version-controlled, auditable, repeatable
04

Observability & Telemetry

Infrastructure you can't see is infrastructure you can't trust — telemetry pipelines and monitoring stacks that make network state visible, queryable, and alertable before incidents become outages.

  • Network telemetry pipeline — Telegraf, InfluxDB, Grafana (TIG stack)
  • BGP advertisement monitoring via BMP (RFC 7854)
  • NetFlow collection at the internet edge — traffic baselining and DDoS detection
  • Service availability monitoring — synthetic checks, alerting, and uptime visibility
05

High Availability & Resilience

A single device failure should be an event in the monitoring system, not an incident for the service desk — resilience is designed in at every layer, then validated by automation.

  • HA firewall clusters with nodes across separate DC rooms or sites — no single device failure interrupts traffic flow
  • WAN path diversity — MPLS edge present at each DC, shared services reachable independently via either site
  • 3rd-site backup architecture — off-site recovery target and archive beyond the primary DC or secondary DC
  • Automation-enforced standby consistency — backup paths and configurations kept in sync with primary, drift-free

How I work

Architecture that's explainable, measurable, and operable once the workshop ends — not just during the design review.

01 Assess

Audit failure modes before design begins. Every bad design is a misread constraint in disguise.

Examines
  • Performance data & failure patterns
  • Team habits & operational load
  • Latent technical debt
  • Budget envelope and business constraints
Not

Surface requirements that ignore how the system is actually operated.

02 Design

Every decision documented — what was chosen, what was rejected, and why. The team in year three deserves to understand what they inherited.

Produces
  • Architecture decision records
  • Network & platform designs
  • Day-two operational runbooks
  • Cost impact documentation — design decisions justified in terms a CTO and CFO can both reason about
Not

Architecture optimised for the design review rather than the operator who inherits it.

03 Enable

A system isn't delivered until the team running it can make changes without fear. Observable state. Self-evident failure modes. No tribal knowledge required.

Produces
  • Automation frameworks & IaC
  • Operational runbooks
  • Knowledge transfer sessions
Not

Documentation nobody reads because the system isn't self-evident enough to run without tribal knowledge.
