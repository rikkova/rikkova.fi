Who I am

I am an Infrastructure Architect with a 9-year trajectory through some of the most demanding infrastructure environments in the world — hyperscale operations at Google, data center transformation at CGI, and cyber defense at Accenture.

Current Focus

Deploying next-generation greenfield network fabrics and modernizing rigid legacy infrastructure. I bridge the gap between high-level architectural vision and execution, ensuring technical debt is engineered out of the equation.

Core Expertise

Advanced networking (BGP, OSPF, VXLAN-EVPN, MPLS, DWDM). Comprehensive data center capacity planning, including bandwidth forecasting, hardware lifecycle management, and scaling topologies to optimize power, cooling, and footprint constraints. Expert at turning manual configurations into scalable, automated workflows using Ansible.

Philosophy

Great architecture requires both resilient networks and informed teams. I invest heavily in mentoring engineers to understand the why behind design decisions, ensuring the systems we build remain sustainable long after deployment.

What I build

My work sits at the layer where infrastructure decisions shape delivery speed, risk posture, and day-two operations.

Platform Architecture

Designing network infrastructure that handles massive traffic volumes reliably. I build systems with automatic failover so they stay available even when hardware breaks or networks go down.

Operational Resilience

Building systems that stay reliable under pressure. This means designing hardware layouts to maximize efficiency while maintaining uptime guarantees, and creating virtual networks that automatically recover from failures.

Infrastructure Automation

Automating infrastructure management so humans don't have to manually configure every device. I've built frameworks that cut manual work by 50% across large device fleets, turning high-risk change processes into repeatable operations anyone can run safely.

How I work

I prefer architecture that is explainable, measurable, and practical to operate after the design workshop ends.

Assess

Before any project starts, I assess the existing environment to understand what's working, what isn't, and where the real risks are. This means looking at performance data, failure patterns, and team habits — not just the surface-level requirements.

Design

I design systems with clear goals from day one. Every component has a purpose, every decision is documented, and nothing gets built without first understanding why it matters. The result is infrastructure that's easier to maintain than the original.

Enable

Good infrastructure doesn't just work — it's something people can use confidently. I build systems that are self-documenting, have clear failure modes, and let teams move fast without fear. The best infrastructure is the one nobody notices because it works exactly as expected.
